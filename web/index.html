    Scientific Collaborator
    AI-assisted literature research

    From a research question to a cited, fact-checked draft

    Ask a question. The assistant searches the scientific literature, finds patterns across papers, writes a structured manuscript, and checks each claim against its source.

    The server has no LLM_API_KEY. Add it to .env and restart the server before starting a run.
    Try:
    What should it write?
    Search depth
    or press Ctrl + Enter

    How it works

    1. ScoutSearches arXiv, PubMed and Semantic Scholar.
    2. DiscoverFinds patterns, conflicts and gaps.
    3. WriteDrafts from facts verified in abstracts.
    4. PolishClearer prose; citations and numbers locked.
    5. VerifyChecks every claim against its source.
    Citations come only from papers it actually found.
    Numbers and quotes are checked against the abstracts.
    It reads abstracts, not full papers. Verify before you rely on it.
